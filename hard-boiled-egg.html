Boiled Egg Recipe

Boiled Egg is one of the most easy recipe there is.

Ingredients:

  • 8 eggs
  • 6 cups water
  • 1/4 cup distilled white vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon salt

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Combine salt,vinegar, and water in a large pot, and bring to boil over high heat.
  3. Add eggs one at a time, being careful not to crack them. Reduce the heat to a gentle boil, and cook for 14 minutes.
  4. Once eggs have cooked, remove them from the how water, and place into a container of ice water or cold, running water. Cool completely, about 15 minutes. Store in the refrigerator up to 1 week.
